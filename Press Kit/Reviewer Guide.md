# Desktop Rave demo — reviewer guide

**Desktop Rave: Idle Audio Club Demo** is a Windows desktop idle-management game whose club reacts to audio playing through the Windows output device. The demo is scheduled to become public on Steam **30 September 2026 at 18:00 CEST / 16:00 UTC**. **No embargo: you may publish screenshots, footage, impressions or coverage whenever you choose.** [Demo page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4987280/) · [full game/wishlist](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3952790/) · [announcement trailer](https://youtu.be/ZPDBeV7jJk4) · [press kit](https://github.com/victorcash/DesktopRavePressKit).

## Preview access

Your individually supplied **Steam demo preview key** unlocks the unreleased demo for your Steam account; it does not make the demo public. Please do not redistribute it. In Steam, choose **Games → Activate a Product on Steam**, enter the key from your private message, then install and launch **Desktop Rave Demo**. Steam may update the preview build before public release.

## Getting started

The demo runs as a transparent Windows desktop overlay. Follow its short first-session onboarding to start your club. **Left-drag the island to move it, hold the right mouse button and drag to rotate the view, and use the mouse wheel to zoom.** WASD or arrow keys also move the camera. Click the floating toolbar to open **Build** and add to your club; **Settings → Audio** contains audio options. To see its audio reaction, play audio through your Windows output device and return to the club. The Music Playlist panel opens curated playlists in external services; these are links, not included music files. **Use synthetic audio** in Settings → Audio is an optional fixed-beat mode if no Windows audio is available; turn it off to resume real system-audio reaction. Windows 10 64-bit is the listed minimum OS; the Steam demo page lists **3 GB** storage.

**Languages:** The Steam demo page lists **30 interface languages**, all checked in the current preview build.

**Known issues:** None known in the tested build as of 25 September. Report a reproducible problem with the Build ID and Windows/audio setup so it can be investigated.

The demo is designed for open-ended idle play; there is no required completion time for a first look. Capture setups for transparent desktop overlays vary, so ask for help with your specific setup if needed.

## Recording and music

You may publish demo footage immediately. The game reacting to music does **not** grant rights to publish that music. The Recommended and Streamer Friendly playlist buttons lead to external services, not music bundled into the game. Independent creators can choose an **actual NCS track** for eligible online video/stream coverage under [NCS's current usage policy](https://ncs.io/usage-policy), including the required artist/track credit; check the official NCS track page and the policy for the intended platform and use. A playlist label alone does not verify every track or grant rights to an organization. Other outlets should use their own cleared music or capture without music. If recording the transparent overlay is difficult, describe your tested capture setup when asking for help rather than assuming one method works for everyone.
