# Desktop Rave demo — questions for coverage

Updated 26 September 2026. [Press-kit home](../README.md) · [Reviewer guide](Reviewer%20Guide.md) · [Press contact](Contact.md)

## When and where can readers play?

The free Windows PC demo is scheduled to open on Steam on **30 September 2026 at 18:00 CEST / 16:00 UTC**. Use the [demo page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4987280/) for access and the [full-game page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3952790/) for wishlists. The full game is scheduled for **11 November 2026**. Check Steam for current availability before publication.

## Can press try it before launch?

Yes, an individually supplied demo preview key can unlock early access. [Request a preview key](mailto:contact@28ducks.com?subject=Desktop%20Rave%20demo%20preview%20request) with your outlet or channel link. A demo key does not unlock the full game. Existing recipients can follow the [activation instructions](Reviewer%20Guide.md#preview-access).

## What does the demo let players do?

Build and upgrade a desktop club, welcome Ravers, earn Vibe and Credits, and see the club react to audio playing through Windows. The club runs in a movable, transparent desktop overlay alongside other apps. A fixed-beat synthetic audio option is available when there is no system audio.

## Where does demo progression stop? Can I keep playing?

The demo's Player Level cap is **9**. After claiming the level-up rewards at that point, players see **Demo Complete!** and can choose **Keep Playing**. The club remains playable at the cap; the completion prompt does not mean the game must close. There is also a one-time wishlist prompt at Player Level 5.

## How long should I set aside?

There is no required completion time for coverage. The [reviewer guide](Reviewer%20Guide.md#suggested-first-look) provides a suggested first-look route. Its time budget is a planning suggestion, not a measured completion time or a promise that every feature unlocks within it.

## Does the demo save? Will its progress carry into the full game?

The demo saves progress automatically and on normal exit. It does not expose the in-game **Save / Load** toolbar panel. **Demo saves do not transfer to the full game**: the two products keep separate saves. Use **Save & Exit** when finishing a session.

## What changes in the full game?

The full game does not have the demo's Player Level 9 cap and includes the in-game **Save / Load** panel for managing clubs. For a question about a specific item or feature, contact [28 Ducks](Contact.md); this kit does not give an item-by-item demo/full-game catalog comparison. No full-game price is stated here.

## Will the demo disappear after the campaign?

This kit does not announce an end date. Check the [demo's Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4987280/) for current availability, or contact [28 Ducks](Contact.md) for a scheduling question.

## Does it come with music?

The game reacts to audio playing through Windows. Its **Music Playlist** buttons open external playlists; those tracks are not bundled into the game. **Settings → Audio → Use synthetic audio** provides an optional fixed-beat mode. See the [recording guide](Recording%20Guide.md) for recording without third-party music.

## What platform and languages are supported?

Windows PC via Steam. The demo's Steam page lists Windows 10 (64-bit), 3 GB of available storage and 30 interface languages. Refer to [Steam's system requirements and language list](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4987280/) for the complete current specifications.
