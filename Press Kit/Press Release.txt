Desktop Rave, the music-powered desktop companion, gets a free Steam demo on 30 September

Use your own music to drive the in-game economy and create your own unique club.

Game descriptions updated 27 September 2026; the announcement date remains 26 September 2026.

Berlin, Germany — 26 September 2026 — 28 Ducks will release a free Steam demo of Desktop Rave: Idle Audio Club for Windows PC on 30 September 2026 at 18:00 CEST / 16:00 UTC. Desktop Rave is an audio-reactive desktop companion and idle management game. Players use their own music to drive the in-game economy and create their own unique club.

Players are the DJ of their own club. Ravers dance to whatever music is playing and earn currency with each beat. Building and upgrading the club combines strategic placement with options for lighting, textures and motion. Players can also interact with Ravers and get feedback on how they are running the club.

The club can be rotated, resized and moved freely to fit the space on the screen, and it keeps running while players use their PC.

The full game is scheduled for 11 November 2026 and can be wishlisted on Steam. The free demo has its own Steam page, linked below.

Screenshots, logos, a demo announcement trailer and a 12-second silent gameplay clip are available in the press kit. There is no coverage embargo. For preview access, questions about the game or additional images, email contact@28ducks.com.
