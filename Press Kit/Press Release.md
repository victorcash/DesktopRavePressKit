# Desktop Rave brings an audio-reactive neon club to Windows desktops with a free Steam demo on 30 September

*28 Ducks' idle-management game lets players build a tiny club that reacts to audio playing on their PC.*

**Berlin, Germany — 26 September 2026** — 28 Ducks will release a free Steam demo of Desktop Rave: Idle Audio Club for Windows PC on 30 September 2026 at 18:00 CEST / 16:00 UTC. The game runs in a transparent desktop overlay, allowing players to grow a neon club alongside their other apps.

Players build and upgrade their club, welcome Ravers, and earn Vibe and Credits as it runs in the background. Audio playing through Windows drives the club's reactions, connecting its Ravers, lighting and progression to what the player is listening to. The borderless club can be moved around the desktop to fit a work or play setup.

The demo also includes an optional fixed-beat synthetic audio mode for play without Windows audio. Its Music Playlist buttons open external playlists; those tracks are not bundled with the game. Steam lists 30 interface languages for the demo.

The full game is scheduled for 11 November 2026 and can be wishlisted on Steam. The free demo has its own Steam page, linked below.

Screenshots, logos, a demo announcement trailer and a 12-second silent gameplay clip are available in the press kit. There is no coverage embargo. Press and creators can contact 28 Ducks about preview access, interviews or additional materials.

## About 28 Ducks

28 Ducks is a game developer and publisher based in Berlin, Germany. It is developing Desktop Rave: Idle Audio Club, a Windows desktop idle-management game built around an audio-reactive neon club.
